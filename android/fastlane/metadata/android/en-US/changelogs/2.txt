A confirmed action now gets its own page: a full-screen terminal that streams
Termux's output, with Copy output and Cancel in the footer.

- Every command runs on that page, so the consent screens stay decisions and the
  output gets the whole screen.
- Check setup has its own page and reports the installed version in the same
  terminal.
- The terminal is rendered by xterm.js, so colours and progress bars from Termux
  survive the trip, and its last line always stays inside the visible area.
